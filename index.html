About me

I’m a multilingual UX designer with a passion for bringing ideas to life through thoughtful, user-centered design.
Passionate about building responsive, accessible experiences, I specialize in modern front-end technologies and creating intuitive user experiences.


Whether building brands from scratch or enhancing established platforms, I’m dedicated to making complex concepts accessible and visually compelling.

My Approach

User-centred & empathy-driven: I uncover real user needs by carefully listening, observing, and analysing behaviour across different touchpoints. By understanding users’ motivations, frustrations, and expectations, I translate insights into meaningful design decisions that prioritise clarity, usability, and trust throughout the experience.

Latest Work

Empathy Map

  • UX Research
  • UX Writing

An empathy map created to explore user comfort, usability, and trust within the EasyJet mobile application experience. Grounded in empathy mapping and user research, this work focuses on understanding users’ emotions, needs, expectations, and pain points throughout their journey in order to deliver supportive, reassuring interactions. By analysing behavioural patterns and emotional responses, the empathy map highlights areas of cognitive effort, uncertainty, and reassurance, providing a foundation for clearer UX design and more intuitive interface decisions. This research-driven approach aims to inform human-centred design solutions that support ease of use, build trust, and help users feel understood and in control.
